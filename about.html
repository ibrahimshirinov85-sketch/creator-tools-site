Why this site exists

Creator Tools started because we kept running into the same problem: every "best AI tool" article online is either written by someone who's never opened the product, or it's a thinly disguised ad for whichever company pays the highest commission.

This site is an attempt to do it differently. We sign up for the tools ourselves, use them for real tasks, and write down what actually happened — the setup friction, the pricing traps, the features that sound good on the landing page but fall apart in daily use.

Who this is for

Freelancers, solopreneurs, and small business owners who don't have a budget for mistakes. If you're deciding between two tools and $20/month actually matters to your business, this site is for you.

How reviews get made

Every review follows the same process: sign up, use the free plan or trial for real work, document pricing and limits honestly, and give a plain verdict — worth it, skip it, or depends on your case. No tool gets a positive review just because it has an affiliate program.

This site contains affiliate links. If you sign up for a tool through one of them, we may earn a commission — at no extra cost to you. It never changes the verdict. Full details on the Disclosure page.

Where this is headed

This site is brand new and growing one review at a time. If there's a tool you'd like tested, that's exactly the kind of thing we want to hear about.
