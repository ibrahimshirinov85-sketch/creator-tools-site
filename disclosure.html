Affiliate Disclosure

Creator Tools is a reader-supported site. When you click certain links on this site and sign up for a product, we may earn a commission from the company behind it. This comes at no additional cost to you — prices are the same whether you use our link or go directly to the company.

How this affects what we publish

Being an affiliate for a tool does not influence whether we recommend it or how we score it. We aim to give the same verdict we would if no commission were involved. If a tool isn't good, we say so — commission or not.

Our testing process

Where possible, we sign up for tools ourselves and test them using their free plan or trial before publishing a review. Screenshots and specific details in our reviews reflect direct, hands-on use of the product at the time of writing.
