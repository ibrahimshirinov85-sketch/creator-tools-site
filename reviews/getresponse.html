GetResponse Review: Is It Worth It in 2026?

GetResponse markets itself as an all-in-one marketing platform — email, automation, landing pages, and a free CRM bundled together. For a solo creator or freelancer trying to avoid paying for five separate tools, that pitch is worth taking seriously. Here's what actually held up.

Pricing

GetResponse offers a free plan that includes email sending to a limited list size, which is genuinely usable for testing the platform before committing. Paid plans scale by list size and unlock automation workflows, webinars, and unlimited landing pages.

What it's good at

The landing page builder and email automation editor are both drag-and-drop and don't require a learning curve to get a working funnel live in an afternoon. For a freelancer who needs "email marketing that works" rather than "email marketing to obsess over," this is a real strength.

Where it falls short

The automation builder gets harder to manage once a workflow has more than a handful of steps — it's built for straightforward sequences, not complex behavioral branching. If your funnel logic gets elaborate, you'll eventually feel the ceiling.

Verdict

Worth it for freelancers and small businesses who want one tool to handle email, a simple funnel, and basic automation without stitching together separate platforms. Less suited to teams that need advanced segmentation or complex multi-branch automation.
